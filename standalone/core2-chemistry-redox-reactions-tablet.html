⚡ Core Redox Rules

• Oxidation: Loss of electrons (LEO) / Increase in O.N.
• Reduction: Gain of electrons (GER) / Decrease in O.N.
• Oxidizing Agent: Accepts e⁻ (gets reduced).
• Reducing Agent: Donates e⁻ (gets oxidized).
• Rusting Formula: 4Fe + 3O₂ + 2xH₂O → 2Fe₂O₃·xH₂O (hydrated ferric oxide).
