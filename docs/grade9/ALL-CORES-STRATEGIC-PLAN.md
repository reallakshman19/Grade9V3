# All Cores Strategic & Architectural Curriculum Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

This document establishes the strategic, pedagogical, and technical architecture for producing **all learner "Cores"** across the Grade 9–10 Foundation curriculum (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics), bridging smoothly to CBSE Class 9/10, Olympiad (NSEJS/PRMO), and Senior Competitive (JEE Main / NEET UG).

### Non-Negotiable Curricular Boundary
In strict adherence to project requirements:
- **IIT-JEE Advanced specialized nomenclature, advanced multivariable/differential calculus, Stokes drag integrals, and arbitrary coaching contrivances are strictly EXCLUDED.**
- Handouts, ready-reference guides, and declarative textbooks use authentic academic terminology: **Grade 9 Foundation · Olympiad (NSEJS) · JEE Main / NEET Foundation**.

## 3. Master Subject & Subtopic Foundation Matrix

Across Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, the curriculum is organized into governed matrix units:

### 3.1 Physics (Grade 9 Core + Foundation Bridges)
1. **Kinematics in 1D** (`MATRIX-PHY-KIN-1D-MOTION`): Position, displacement, velocity-time graphs, equations of motion under constant acceleration, vertical motion under gravity.
2. **Newton's Laws of Motion** (`MATRIX-PHY-NLM-FIRST-LAW`): Inertia, momentum, Newton's 3 laws, normal reaction, friction (static/kinetic/limiting).
3. **Gravitation & Flotation** (`MATRIX-PHY-GRAV-UNIVERSAL-LAW`): Universal gravitation, acceleration due to gravity $g$, mass vs weight, thrust, pressure, Archimedes' principle, relative density.
4. **Work, Energy & Power** (`MATRIX-PHY-WORK-ENERGY-POWER`): Mechanical work, kinetic & potential energy, work-energy theorem, conservation of mechanical energy, power.
5. **Sound & Waves** (`MATRIX-PHY-SOUND`): Longitudinal waves, wave speed $v = f\lambda$, reflection of sound, echoes, ultrasound.
6. **Simple Machines** (`MATRIX-PHY-SIMPLE-MACHINES`): Mechanical advantage, velocity ratio, efficiency, levers, single/compound pulleys.
7. **Vector Algebra Foundations** (`MATRIX-PHY-VECTOR-REPRESENTATION` & `MATRIX-PHY-VEC-ADD-SUB`): Vector vs scalar, tail-to-head triangle law, parallelogram law, vector subtraction.
8. **Vector Resolution & Unit Vectors** (`MATRIX-PHY-VEC-DIRECTION-UNIT`): Cartesian components, unit vectors $\hat{i}, \hat{j}$, direction cosines, resolving forces.
9. **Motion in 2D / Projectiles** (`MATRIX-PHY-KIN-2D-MOTION`): Horizontal launch from a height, ground-to-ground oblique launch, apex state, trajectory equation.
10. **Momentum Transfer & Collisions** (`MATRIX-PHY-NLM-MOMENTUM-TRANSFER`): Impulse, 1D elastic collisions, explosions, recoil velocity.
11. **Relative Velocity in a Plane** (`MATRIX-PHY-RELATIVE-MOTION`): Relative velocity in 1D & 2D, river-boat crossing (shortest time vs shortest path), rain-man problems.
12. **Uniform Circular Motion** (`MATRIX-PHY-CIRCULAR-MOTION`): Speed, angular speed $\omega$, centripetal acceleration $a_c = v^2/r$, centripetal force.

### 3.2 Chemistry (Grade 9–10 Foundation & Bridge)
1. **Mole Concept & Stoichiometry** (`MATRIX-CHM-MOLE-CONCEPT`): Atomic & molecular mass, mole conversions, empirical & molecular formulas, limiting reagents, eudiometry.
2. **Chemical Bonding & Molecular Structure** (`MATRIX-CHM-CHEMICAL-BONDING`): Ionic & covalent bonding, octet rule, Lewis structures, polar bonds, basic VSEPR shapes.
3. **Redox Reactions** (`MATRIX-CHM-REDOX-REACTIONS`): Oxidation numbers, oxidizing/reducing agents, electron transfer, ion-electron balancing method.
4. **Gaseous State** (`MATRIX-CHM-GASEOUS-STATE`): Boyle's law, Charles's law, Gay-Lussac's law, Avogadro's law, Ideal gas equation $PV = nRT$.

### 3.3 Mathematics (Foundation Bridges)
1. **Linear & Quadratic Systems** (`MATRIX-MTH-LINEQ-POLYNOMIALS`): Simultaneous linear equations, quadratic factorization, roots, discriminant.
2. **Coordinate Geometry** (`MATRIX-MTH-COORD-GEOMETRY`): Distance formula, section formula, slope of a line, linear Cartesian equations.
3. **Trigonometric Foundations** (`MATRIX-MTH-TRIG-FOUNDATIONS`): $\sin, \cos, \tan$, special angles ($0^\circ, 30^\circ, 45^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ$), Pythagorean identities.

## 4. Immediate Core 1A Implementation: Motion in a Plane (20 Modular Lessons)

The textbook layout strictly follows the 1-concept-per-landscape-page pedagogical standard:

```
§1 Foundation: Vector Components & Independence
  • Lesson 01 (D1): Cartesian Coordinate Choice & Component Resolution
  • Lesson 02 (D1): Principle of Physical Independence of Perpendicular Axes
  • Lesson 03 (D2): Vector Velocity Addition & Magnitude Calculation
  • Lesson 04 (D2): Uniform Acceleration in 2D with Orthogonal Decoupling

§2 Horizontal Projectile Motion (Launch from a Height)
  • Lesson 05 (D1): The Horizontal Launch: Zero Initial Vertical Velocity
  • Lesson 06 (D1): Fall Time Independence from Horizontal Speed (t = sqrt(2h/g))
  • Lesson 07 (D2): Horizontal Range (R = u*sqrt(2h/g)) & Landing Velocity Vector
  • Lesson 08 (D3): Striking Angle & Instantaneous Trajectory Equation (y = g x^2 / [2 u^2])

§3 Oblique Projectile Motion (Ground-to-Ground)
  • Lesson 09 (D1): Velocity Decomposition at Launch (ux = u cos theta, uy = u sin theta)
  • Lesson 10 (D1): Time of Ascent & Apex Dynamics (vy = 0, t_up = u sin theta / g)
  • Lesson 11 (D2): Total Time of Flight & Parabolic Symmetry (T = 2 u sin theta / g)
  • Lesson 12 (D2): Maximum Height Derivation & Velocity at Apex (H = u^2 sin^2 theta / [2g])
  • Lesson 13 (D2): Horizontal Range Formula & 45-degree Maximum Condition (R = u^2 sin 2theta / g)
  • Lesson 14 (D2): Complementary Launch Angles (theta and 90 - theta)
  • Lesson 15 (D3): Speed Codex Link: R = 4H cot theta and Invariants
  • Lesson 16 (D3): Parabolic Trajectory Equation (y = x tan theta [1 - x/R])

§4 Elevation Trajectories & Wall Clearance
  • Lesson 17 (D2): Projectile Launched from a Cliff at an Angle
  • Lesson 18 (D3): Target Clearance: Minimum Launch Velocity to Clear a Barrier

§5 Planar Relative Motion & Uniform Circular Motion
  • Lesson 19 (D2): Relative Velocity in a Plane: Vector Subtraction (v_A/B = v_A - v_B)
  • Lesson 20 (D3): Uniform Circular Motion as 2D Motion with Centripetal Acceleration (a_c = v^2/r)
```

```

       │
       ▼

       │
       ├─ Draft compact orientation notes and governing relations
       ├─ Extract geometric and algebraic invariants (Speed Hacks)
       └─ Render 4–6 page Ready Reference Handout (Landscape PDF)
       │
       ▼
[Phase 3: Core 1A Declarative Textbook]
       │
       ├─ Map 15–25 single-page modular lessons (D1, D2, D3 tiers)
       ├─ Build precision scaled SVG diagrams with coordinate axes
       ├─ Formulate completed step-by-step derivations
       └─ Compile 30–40 page Landscape Master PDF
       │
       ▼
[Phase 4: Core 1B Socratic Reconstruction]
       │
       ├─ Author Predict → Attempt → Reconstruct → Diagnose → Repair sequences
       └─ Embed boundary-limit confirmation checks
       │
       ▼
[Phase 5: Core 2A & 2B Question Mastery]
       │
       ├─ Core 2A: Tag crux moves, author cognitive scaffolds (Represent/Connect/Execute)
       ├─ Core 2B: Build transfer problems across the 4 changed-demand dimensions
       └─ Run 11-gate machine audit test suite to guarantee 100% zero defects
```
