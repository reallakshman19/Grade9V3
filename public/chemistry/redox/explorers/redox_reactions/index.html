Redox Foundations · See the electrons before using the rules

GRADE 9 BRIDGE

Start with one causal idea: electrons move from a donor to an acceptor. Oxidation means electron loss; reduction means electron gain. Oxidation numbers are then introduced as a formal bookkeeping system for tracking that change—not as literal atomic charges.

This bridge is learner support only. The overall suite and its 20-question bank remain an unbound JEE extension, not a Grade-9 certification claim.

Text invariant: Zn loses 2e⁻ → Zn²⁺; Cu²⁺ gains 2e⁻ → Cu. Electron loss and gain must match.

Oxidation-State Bookkeeping · Structure-Aware Exceptions

Use the ordinary rules first, then stress their boundaries. Peroxides require O = -1, while mixed-valence and multi-centre structures can make a formula-average oxidation number different from the atom-by-atom formal assignment.

Begin with the causal picture: the reducing agent donates electrons and is oxidized; the oxidizing agent accepts those same electrons and is reduced. Mnemonics come after the mechanism.

Text invariant: electrons lost by the reducing agent equal electrons gained by the oxidizing agent.
