Universal Meta-Problem-Solving Verification Filters
⚡ Dimensional Homogeneity Filter
Rule: Every additive term and both sides of an equality must possess identical SI dimensions [M^a L^b T^c].
Speed Impact: Instantly eliminates 1 to 2 incorrect multiple-choice options before writing any physics derivation.
⚡ Limiting Boundary-Case Stress Test
Rule: Test asymptotic parameter extremes (θ→0°, θ→90°, μ→0, μ→∞, m→0, M→∞).
Speed Impact: If an acceleration formula on an incline does not yield a = g sinθ when μ→0, or a = g when θ→90°, reject it immediately.
⚡ Redox Electron Charge Conservation Balance
Rule: Total ionic charges on the reactant side must strictly match total ionic charges on the product side (Σ q_initial = Σ q_final).
Speed Impact: Validates redox equation balancing in 5 seconds without re-counting intermediate hydrogen and oxygen atoms.
⚡ Stoichiometric Mass & Atom Invariance
Rule: In closed chemical reactions, total grams of reactants equal total grams of products (Σ m_reactants = Σ m_products).
Speed Impact: Cracks complex multi-stage mixture and gas stoichiometry problems via global mass conservation.

Galileo's Odd-Integer Free-Fall Law INVARIANT
s1 : s2 : s3 : ... : sn = 1 : 3 : 5 : ... : (2n - 1)
Over consecutive equal time intervals Δt from rest under constant acceleration a, distance sn = ½ a (Δt)² (2n - 1).
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Avoid quadratic displacement equations. If distance in the 1st second is h, distance in the 3rd second is identically 5h.
DOMAIN: Initial velocity must be strictly zero (u = 0) and acceleration must be constant.
TRAP: Never apply if body has non-zero initial speed (u ≠ 0). In that case, sn = u Δt + ½ a (Δt)² (2n - 1).
🎯 DRILL: Free Fall from Tower (NEET (UG) / JEE Main Core) ⚡ 10s vs 60s
Distance in 4th second for dropped stone = (2×4 - 1) × (½ g) = 7 × 4.9 = 34.3 m directly.
Average Speed for Segmented Journeys INVARIANT
Equal Distances: vavg = 2 v1 v2 / (v1 + v2)  |  Equal Times: vavg = (v1 + v2) / 2
Equal distances mandate harmonic mean (time varies inversely with speed); equal times mandate arithmetic mean.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
By AM-HM inequality, vavg, time ≥ vavg, dist with equality iff v1 = v2.
DOMAIN: Applicable strictly when motion occurs along a single straight direction without turning points.
TRAP: Round-trip journeys always have equal distances (use harmonic mean), not equal times.
🎯 DRILL: Round-Trip Commute (Grade 9 Foundation / NEET) ⚡ 5s vs 35s
Vehicle travels at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h: vavg = 2(40)(60)/(40 + 60) = 48 km/h (never 50 km/h!).
Stopping Distance & Velocity Scaling Rule INVARIANT
dstop = v₀² / (2a)   ∝   v₀²,    tstop = v₀ / a   ∝   v₀
From v² = u² - 2as, setting v = 0 gives stopping distance directly proportional to the square of initial speed.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
If speed is multiplied by factor k, stopping distance multiplies by k². Doubling speed (k=2) quadruples stopping distance (4×).
DOMAIN: Braking acceleration a must remain constant throughout retardation.
TRAP: Stopping distance scales as v², but stopping time scales linearly as v. Never confuse the two exponents.
🎯 DRILL: Automobile Emergency Braking (JEE Main / Foundation) ⚡ 5s vs 40s
Car at 50 km/h stops in 12 m. At 150 km/h (3× speed), stopping distance = 12 × 3² = 12 × 9 = 108 m instantly.
Freeze-Frame Relative Collision & Overtaking INVARIANT
vA/B = vA - vB,    aA/B = aA - aB,    smin = vrel² / (2 |arel|)
Transforming into target B's reference frame reduces a two-body coupled differential system into a single 1D stopping calculation.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
To prevent rear-end collision when vehicle A approaches vehicle B: minimum safe initial gap is smin = (vA - vB)² / (2 adecel).
DOMAIN: Relative acceleration arel must remain constant throughout the braking maneuver.
TRAP: In opposing motion (head-on), remember vector signs: vrel = vA - (-vB) = vA + vB.
🎯 DRILL: Highway Relative Tracking (JEE Main 2026 · Shift 2 · Q27) ⚡ 15s vs 75s
Cars A (100 km/h) & B (80 km/h): passenger in B observes relative speed vrel = 100 - 80 = +20 km/h forward.

MOD-02 2D Motion & Projectile Dynamics Arsenal
MOTION_2D_PROJECTILE 4 SPEED HACKS
The Factored Trajectory Equation INVARIANT
y = x tanθ (1 - x / R)
Factoring x tanθ from the standard parabola y = x tanθ - [g x² / (2 v₀² cos²θ)] directly exposes horizontal range R = (v₀² sin 2θ) / g.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
When given barrier coordinates (x₁, y₁), never expand cos²θ. Use tanθ = y₁ / [x₁ (1 - x₁ / R)] for an instant 1-line solve.
DOMAIN: Launch and landing points must be at the same vertical datum (level horizontal terrain).
TRAP: Do not use when launching onto an elevated terrace (elevation mismatch breaks level range symmetry).
🎯 DRILL: Target Obstacle Clearance (JEE Main Core Kinematics) ⚡ 25s vs 110s
Passes through (x=5m, y=1m) with range R=20m: 1 = 5 tanθ (1 - 5/20) = 5 tanθ (3/4) ⇒ tanθ = 4/15.
Complementary Launch Angles Invariants INVARIANT
Rθ = R90°-θ  |  R = 4 √(H1 H2)  |  t1 t2 = 2R / g  |  H1 + H2 = v₀² / (2g)
Angles θ and 90°-θ yield identical sin 2θ. Cross products of heights and times cancel trigonometric factors identically.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Given maximum heights H₁ and H₂ of two complementary shots with identical speed, Range is 4√(H₁ H₂) without computing speed v₀ or angle θ.
DOMAIN: Initial speeds must be identical, launched over horizontal ground.
TRAP: Remember factor 4 in R = 4√(H₁ H₂); forgetting factor 4 is the most common arithmetic trap.
🎯 DRILL: Dual Trajectory Heights (NEET (UG) / JEE Main Invariants) ⚡ 10s vs 75s
H₁ = 20 m, H₂ = 45 m. Range R = 4 √(20 × 45) = 4 √(900) = 4 × 30 = 120 m directly.
Horizontal Tower Launch & Free-Fall Decoupling INVARIANT
tfall = √(2h / g),    vx = R / √(2h / g),    vimpact = √(vx² + 2gh)
Vertical motion is pure free fall from rest independent of horizontal launch speed.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
For horizontal launch from height h, horizontal speed is directly v = R / t = R / √(2h / g).
DOMAIN: Initial vertical velocity must be zero (purely horizontal takeoff).
TRAP: Do not treat landing distances as proportional to velocity if vertical fall heights differ.
🎯 DRILL: Cliff Projectile Landing (Grade 9 Foundation / NEET) ⚡ 15s vs 80s
h = 20m, lands at R = 40m. t = √(2(20)/10) = 2.0s ⇒ Horizontal launch speed v₀ = 40 / 2.0 = 20 m/s.
Maximum Range & Apex Energy Invariants INVARIANT
At θ = 45°: Rmax = v₀² / g = 4 Hmax  |  Kapex = K₀ cos²θ
Maximum range occurs when sin 2θ = 1 (θ = 45°). Vertical velocity at apex vanishes; horizontal velocity v₀ cosθ remains invariant.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
At maximum range angle (45°), Range is always exactly 4 times the maximum height (R = 4H). Apex kinetic energy is exactly half (½ K₀).
DOMAIN: Constant gravitational acceleration g without atmospheric air drag.
TRAP: Kinetic energy at highest point is not zero; it equals ½ m (v₀ cosθ)², which is non-zero for any oblique angle.
🎯 DRILL: Apex Kinetic Energy Fraction (NEET (UG) / Foundation) ⚡ 10s vs 50s
If kinetic energy at apex is half of initial KE: cos²θ = ½ ⇒ cosθ = 1/√2 ⇒ θ = 45° (Maximum range angle!).

MOD-03 Newton's Laws, Friction Engine & Constraint Relations
MECHANICS_NLM 4 SPEED HACKS
Whole-System Acceleration Heuristic INVARIANT
asys = (Σ Fdriving - Σ Fopposing) / Σ minertial
Internal constraint forces (tensions, mutual normal contact forces) form action-reaction pairs that cancel over the total free body.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Never write simultaneous 3-variable equations for Atwood or connected incline systems. One line gives acceleration directly.
DOMAIN: All interconnected components must share identical scalar acceleration magnitude along the constraint path.
TRAP: Include all moving masses in denominator, including pulleys if they have non-negligible rotational inertia.
🎯 DRILL: Standard Atwood Machine (NEET (UG) / Foundation) ⚡ 10s vs 60s
Masses 3 kg & 2 kg over smooth pulley: a = (3 - 2)g / (3 + 2) = g / 5 = 2.0 m/s². Tension T = 2(3)(2)g / 5 = 24 N.
Inclined Plane Friction & Time Ratio Scaling INVARIANT
a = g (sinθ - μ cosθ)  |  If trough = n · tsmooth ⇒ μ = tanθ [ 1 - (1 / n²) ]
From s = ½ a t², acceleration varies inversely as t². Thus a_rough / a_smooth = 1 / n², yielding μ in one line.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
If a block takes n times longer to slide down a rough incline than a smooth incline of angle θ, μ = tanθ (1 - 1/n²) directly.
DOMAIN: Incline angle θ must exceed the angle of repose (tanθ > μ) so that motion actually occurs from rest.
TRAP: Factor is (1 - 1/n²), never (1 - 1/n).
🎯 DRILL: Incline Sliding Time Ratio (JEE Main 2026 · Shift 2 · Q46) ⚡ 15s vs 90s
Takes twice as long (n=2) on 45° rough plane: μ = tan 45° [1 - 1/(2²)] = 1 × (1 - 1/4) = 0.75 in 5 seconds.
Pulling vs. Pushing Friction Margin INVARIANT
Npull = mg - F sinθ  (fmax = μ Npull)  |  Npush = mg + F sinθ  (fmax = μ Npush)
Pulling has an upward vertical component reducing normal force; pushing has a downward vertical component increasing normal force.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Normal shift is exactly ΔN = 2 F sinθ. Pushing jams completely when tanθ = μ (force required blows up to infinity!).
DOMAIN: Force applied at angle θ to horizontal on rough horizontal surface.
TRAP: If pulling force is very large (F sinθ > mg), the body lifts off the surface (N = 0, friction vanishes completely).
🎯 DRILL: Lawn Roller Dynamics (Grade 9 Foundation / NEET) ⚡ 10s vs 50s
Pulling reduces normal force by F sinθ, decreasing friction resistance f = μ N. Pushing adds F sinθ, increasing resistance.
Two-Block Slipping Threshold Rule INVARIANT
amax = μs g,    Fthresh = (M + m) μs g
Friction between the blocks is the sole force accelerating the upper block m; its maximum possible acceleration is μ_s g.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Compute system acceleration a_sys = F / (M + m). If a_sys ≤ μ_s g, they move together without slip. If a_sys > μ_s g, slip occurs.
DOMAIN: Smooth horizontal ground under lower block M; friction acts only at mutual interface.
TRAP: Once slipping begins, static friction μ_s must immediately be replaced by kinetic friction μ_k.
🎯 DRILL: Block-on-Block Slip Threshold (JEE Main / Foundation) ⚡ 15s vs 75s
m=2kg on M=3kg, μ_s=0.4: a_max = 0.4(10) = 4 m/s². Maximum pulling force without slipping is F_thresh = (2 + 3)(4) = 20 N.

MOD-04 Mole Concept, Solutions & Stoichiometric Mastery
MOLE_STOICHIOMETRY 4 SPEED HACKS
Molarity-to-Molality Direct Conversion INVARIANT
m = 1000 M / (1000 d - M Mw)    |    d = (M / 1000) [ (1000 / m) + Mw ]
In 1000 mL solution: total mass = 1000 d grams, solute mass = M M_w grams, solvent mass = (1000 d - M M_w) / 1000 kg.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Eliminates the tedious 2-page 'Assume 1 L solution' algebra. Direct plug-and-chug with density d in g/mL.
DOMAIN: Density d must be in g/mL (or g/cm³). Solute molar mass M_w in g/mol.
TRAP: Ensure d is in g/mL, not kg/m³. If given in kg/m³, divide by 1000 first.
🎯 DRILL: Concentration Unit Interconversion (JEE Main / NEET (UG) Core) ⚡ 20s vs 90s
2.0 M aqueous urea (Mw=60) with d=1.06 g/mL: m = 1000(2) / [1000(1.06) - 2(60)] = 2000 / [1060 - 120] = 2000 / 940 = 2.13 m.
Alligation Cross for Binary Mixture Molar Mass INVARIANT
n₁ / n₂ = |M₂ - M_avg| / |M₁ - M_avg|
M_avg = (n₁ M₁ + n₂ M₂) / (n₁ + n₂) rearranged gives n₁ (M_avg - M₁) = n₂ (M₂ - M_avg).
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Solves isotope natural abundances, binary alloy compositions, and gas mixture ratios in 5 seconds via cross subtraction.
DOMAIN: Applies strictly to binary (two-component) mixtures.
TRAP: Alligation gives mole ratio for molar properties, but mass ratio for mass-specific properties (like mass percentages).
🎯 DRILL: Partial Pressures in Gas Mixture (NEET (UG) 2020 · Set E1 · Q110) ⚡ 15s vs 60s
7g N₂ (0.25 mol) & 8g Ar (0.20 mol): n_ratio = 5:4. Mole fraction of Ar = 4/9. Partial pressure P_Ar = (4/9)(27 bar) = 12 bar.
Faraday's Electrolysis Direct Electron Factor INVARIANT
1 Faraday = 96,500 C ≡ 1 mole of electrons  |  Mass m = (M / z) (Q / F)
Reduction of 1 mole of M^(z+) requires exactly z moles of electrons (z Faradays of charge).
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Never use complex electrochemical equivalent formulas. Simply use: Moles deposited = Faradays / z.
DOMAIN: 100% current efficiency assumed unless specified otherwise.
TRAP: Identify cation charge z carefully: e.g., Cu²+ has z=2, but Cu+ (cuprous) has z=1.
🎯 DRILL: Electrolytic Deposition of Calcium (NEET (UG) 2020 · Set E1 · Q106) ⚡ 5s vs 40s
Ca²+ + 2e⁻ → Ca. Molar mass Ca = 40 g/mol ⇒ 40g requires 2 F. Therefore 20g requires exactly 1.0 F.
Limiting Reagent Dimensionless Ratio Test INVARIANT
Ri = ni / νi    (νi = stoichiometric coefficient)
Reaction progress is bounded by the reactant with the minimum value of moles divided by its stoichiometric number.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Divide initial moles of each reactant by its balanced coefficient. Smallest ratio is strictly the Limiting Reagent (LR).
DOMAIN: Reaction must go to completion (irreversible stoichiometry).
TRAP: Never compare raw masses or raw moles directly; always divide by stoichiometric coefficients first.
🎯 DRILL: Haber Ammonia Synthesis (Grade 9 Foundation / NEET) ⚡ 10s vs 50s
N₂ + 3 H₂ → 2 NH₃ with 2 mol N₂ and 3 mol H₂. R_N = 2/1 = 2; R_H = 3/3 = 1. H₂ is LR (ratio 1 < 2), yielding 2 mol NH₃.

MOD-05 Redox Mastery, n-Factor Matrix & Rapid Titrations
REDOX_CHEMISTRY 4 SPEED HACKS
The 'BAN' Mnemonic for Permanganate (KMnO₄) INVARIANT
Basic (strongly alkaline): n = 1  |  Acidic: n = 5  |  Neutral / weakly basic: n = 3
Acidic: MnO₄⁻ + 5e⁻ → Mn²⁺ (+7→+2). Neutral: MnO₄⁻ + 3e⁻ → MnO₂ (+7→+4). Strongly Basic: MnO₄⁻ + 1e⁻ → MnO₄²⁻ (+7→+6).
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Remember keyword 'BAN 1-5-3' (Basic=1, Acidic=5, Neutral=3) to instantly write equivalent weights without balancing half-cells.
DOMAIN: Acidic medium must use dilute H₂SO₄, never concentrated HCl (which oxidizes Cl⁻ to Cl₂).
TRAP: In neutral or faintly alkaline medium, n = 3 (forms MnO₂), not n = 5.
🎯 DRILL: Permanganate Equivalent Weight (NEET (UG) / JEE Main Core) ⚡ 5s vs 45s
Equivalent weight of KMnO₄ in acid is M/5; in neutral medium (forming MnO₂) it is M/3; in strongly basic medium it is M/1.
Dichromate Acidic Invariant INVARIANT
Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 14H⁺ + 6e⁻ → 2Cr³⁺ + 7H₂O    (n = 2 × |6 - 3| = 6)
Two chromium atoms per formula unit each undergo a 3-electron reduction from +6 to +3.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Equivalent weight of K₂Cr₂O₇ in acidic medium is always strictly M / 6.
DOMAIN: Operates as an oxidant in acidic solution; in alkaline medium it hydrolyzes to yellow chromate CrO₄²⁻.
TRAP: Multiply change in oxidation state per atom (3) by number of Cr atoms in formula unit (2) = 6. Forgetting factor 2 is fatal.
🎯 DRILL: Dichromate Charge Transfer (JEE Main / NEET) ⚡ 10s vs 45s
Complete reduction of 1 mol K₂Cr₂O₇ to Cr³⁺ in acidic medium consumes exactly 6 Faradays (6 × 96,500 C) of charge.
Parallel Resistor Rule for Disproportionation INVARIANT
neq = (nox · nred) / (nox + nred)    ⇔    1 / neq = 1 / nox + 1 / nred
Conserving electrons across simultaneously oxidized and reduced branches yields equivalent weight E = M (1/n_ox + 1/n_red).
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Identical structure to parallel resistors. Disproportionation n-factor is computed in 5 seconds without writing full balanced stoichiometry.
DOMAIN: Applicable strictly when a single reactant disproportionates into two distinct redox products.
TRAP: Do not simply add n_ox + n_red; addition is only valid when two different elements in the same compound both oxidize.
🎯 DRILL: Chlorine Gas Disproportionation (JEE Main / NEET Redox) ⚡ 15s vs 70s
Cl₂ + 2 OH⁻ → Cl⁻ (n_red = 1) + ClO⁻ (n_ox = 1). n_eq = (1×1)/(1+1) = 0.5. Equivalent weight of Cl₂ is M / 0.5 = 2M.
The Golden Law of Equivalents (Titration Shortcut) INVARIANT
(N₁ V₁)oxidant = (N₂ V₂)reductant    ⇔    n₁ · moles₁ = n₂ · moles₂
One equivalent of any oxidizing agent completely consumes exactly one equivalent of any reducing agent.
⚡ In-Exam Speed Hack
Never balance full complex redox equations during exam conditions. Multiply moles by respective n-factors and equate directly.
DOMAIN: All reactants must react completely without side reactions.
TRAP: In FeC₂O₄ (ferrous oxalate), both Fe²⁺ (1e⁻) and C₂O₄²⁻ (2e⁻) oxidize, so n-factor is 1 + 2 = 3!
🎯 DRILL: Oxalic Acid Permanganate Titration (Grade 9-10 Olympiad / NEET) ⚡ 15s vs 80s
Oxalic acid (H₂C₂O₄, n=2) titrated with acidified KMnO₄ (n=5): 2 × moles_ox = 5 × moles_perm ⇒ 5 mol oxalic acid requires 2 mol KMnO₄.
